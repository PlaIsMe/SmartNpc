# Dangerous reaction retreat

This checkout is Forge 1.20.1 / Java 17. Sources are `entity/goal/DangerousReactionGoal.java` and shared `entity/ai/DangerousReactionAi.java`.

The Annoying Villagers `DangerousReactionGoal` / `DangerousReaction` design was reviewed as a reference for separating danger conditions and response. Smart NPC implements navigation-based retreat locally, with no Annoying Villagers dependency, custom reaction animation, repeated velocity impulses, delayed jumps, or wall placement.

The standalone goal owns MOVE/LOOK for nearby primed TNT and swelling/ignited creepers. Normal creepers use a six-block starting radius, charged creepers ten, and TNT eight. It continues two blocks farther for hysteresis, then releases movement so healing can start. These are desired avoidance margins, not a guarantee of surviving explosions. Combat targets remain assigned; the goal does not recruit enemies or clear targeting. Active eating retains movement ownership and invokes the same helper to avoid explosives and retreat from its combat threat.

Explosion selection has a UUID-staggered ten-tick cache, an explicit short-fuse emergency exception to ordinary twenty-tick work scans. Queries inspect nearby loaded entities only. Known threat liveness and continuation are O(1). Explosives within the retreat margin outrank healing threats, and every admitted scan can replace a retained explosive with a closer one relative to its danger radius. Healing threats require healing or health below 70%, exclude allies/creative/spectator players, and select the nearest valid target, recent attacker, or twenty-tick cached cautious-trait result. Distant explosives remain a fallback when no healing threat exists; they must not force a safe-distance hold beside a closer attacker.

Retreat planning attempts at most two bounded paths per twenty ticks, rejects candidate and actual path endpoints that do not increase distance, and stops old navigation on threat changes or failed planning. Cached nearby explosives are also checked: candidate and actual endpoints must stay outside each other explosive's margin, or at least not move closer if already inside it. This avoids demanding an impossible immediately safe endpoint when surrounded. Path deadlines survive interruptions. Outside healing, safe distance stops the helper and faces the threat. During healing it continues retreating from a valid threat until food use finishes. Both ordinary healing and explosive retreat now use sprint navigation capped at 0.79 to preserve the player-like flee speed correction. Item/food ownership remains in the eating goal.

Runtime checks should cover TNT during eating, normal and charged igniting creepers, explosion disappearance, inaccessible escape routes, allies/creative players, healing completion and combat resumption with and without Epic Fight.

Retreat routing first checks a small fan of loaded grounded stands four blocks away (level, one up, or one down), with a random fallback. It builds at most two paths per planning slice and rotates past failed directions. An unfinished safe route can resume after navigation is interrupted without another search. Inspector detail distinguishes a chosen retreat from a blocked escape route. These mechanics apply with and without Epic Fight; the EAT StaticAnimation does not impose a movement lock.
